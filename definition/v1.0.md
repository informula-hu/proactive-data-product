# Proactive Data Product – Definition v1.0

Version 1.0 · first published 5 November 2026, Budapest · publisher: INFORMULA Kft. · licence: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)  

This page is the permanent text of version 1.0. It is not edited; changes are published as a new version.

## 1. Definition

> A **proactive data product** is a real-time data product of a business entity – customer, contract, transaction, interaction, risk or consent – with a named owner and a contract. It not only serves its current state on request (**state view**), but also publishes business-meaningful events on its own (**event view**) – to people, systems and AI agents.

## 2. One product, two views

There is no separate “basic” and “proactive” data product. It is one product, owned by one domain, with two views.

| View | Mode | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **State view** | queried | The current state of the entity. |
| **Event view** | published | Business events, as they happen – to people, systems and channels, and AI agents. |

## 3. Four kinds of business events

The event view carries events the business understands, not “record updated”. Several of them are sent by no single source system; they only exist once the entity is seen as a whole, over time.

| # | Kind | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **State transition** | Something moved from an old state to a new one. | Loan application: submitted → approved. |
| 2 | **Threshold or time window** | An aggregate crossed a limit within a period. | Third month in a row at the data cap. |
| 3 | **Time-based** | A date in the entity's own story arrives. | 18th birthday. Maturity in 30 days. |
| 4 | **Absence** | Something expected did not happen. | The regular salary credit is missing. |

## 4. Boundary: data product and situation

Facts belong to the product. Decisions belong to the situation.

| Proactive data product | Situation |
|---|---|
| Facts about one entity: everything that is true about the entity regardless of who uses it – including time-based and absence events from the entity's own history. Maintained by its domain, whether or not anyone consumes it today. | A decision across products: combines events and states of several products and decides whom to contact, with what, when – and whether it is allowed. It carries purpose, channel, consent, contact rules, a metric and a business owner. |

Test: if another situation would use it too, it belongs to the data product. If it decides about contacting a customer, it belongs to the situation.

## 5. Prior art

The ingredients are not new: business objects and business events in enterprise systems; domain events in domain-driven design; complex event processing; governed, owned data products (Patil 2012; Dehghani 2019, 2020; ODCS; ODPS v1.0, 2025).

The combination is: a business entity that spans systems, not one system's view of it; business-meaningful events; absence, time and threshold events no source system sends; state and events in one product, with owner, contract and version; built for people, channels and AI agents alike.
